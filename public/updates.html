What this is

Your private career-search command center

Job Portal brings personalized job discovery, resume preparation, application tracking, follow-ups, and interview readiness into one candidate-controlled workspace. It uses your verified profile and source resume as the factual foundation and never treats an attempted application as successfully submitted without confirmation.

How to use it

A practical path from profile to interview

  1. Complete Profile & Resume: add your identity, preferences, qualifications, work authorization, source resume, and approved answers.
  2. Discover: review ranked jobs and their match explanations; save, hide, report, or focus each role.
  3. Focus: prioritize the best roles, record recruiter/contact details, and schedule follow-ups.
  4. Prepare: choose Tailor & Apply so Hagent creates the documents and a separate reviewer checks quality and truthfulness.
  5. Respond: use the Action Center for login, CAPTCHA, unanswered-question, and unsupported-portal steps.
  6. Track: follow every application through preparing, ready, action-required, and applied stages.
  7. Train: build the interview roadmap, practice, record interviews, and manage offers.
What's new

Cleaner navigation, stronger workflow, one visual system

The current release combines a focused sidebar, main-area profile and resume editing, follow-up planning, interview training, an Action Center, and the charcoal/zinc/violet theme used throughout this version center.

What it can do

From job discovery to interview readiness

The portal brings matching, application preparation, quality review, tracking, follow-ups, employer links, interviews, and offers into one candidate-controlled workspace.

Discover & match

Aggregate jobs from public APIs and employer boards, rank them against your complete profile, explain match strengths and gaps, and save, hide, report, or focus the roles that matter.

Prepare with Hagent

Tailor a resume and cover letter for a selected job, then run a separate truthfulness and quality review before the application is allowed to continue.

Organize the search

Track applications, follow-ups, recruiter notes, alerts, messages, interviews, events, offers, company research, saved searches, and candidate-approved screening answers.

Get interview-ready

Focus priority jobs and follow a seven-step roadmap covering company research, requirement mapping, STAR stories, technical practice, questions, mock interviews, and logistics.

Automation status

Operational discovery, matching, Hagent tailoring, independent review, tracking, alerts, follow-ups, and interview preparation. Connector required real third-party auto-submission, authenticated portal sessions, CAPTCHA handling, and unsupported employer systems.

Version levels

Major versions change workflows or data structures. Minor versions add compatible features. Patch versions contain compatible fixes.

Maturity levels

Stable is production-ready. Controlled is reviewed documentation. Beta works behind an integration boundary and may require manual action.

Known limitations in v3.5.0

  • Automatic external submission requires a configured ATS/browser submission runner.
  • Login, CAPTCHA, unsupported portals, and unanswered legal questions remain Action Required.
  • The ATS score is an estimate; real applicant tracking systems differ, and it cannot honestly reach a target when a job needs skills the resume does not show.
  • AI bullet rewording is off unless the server sets CAREEROPS_AI_KEY, and every suggestion is checked against hard rules before it is shown.
  • An application is marked Applied only after a real confirmation reference or explicit user confirmation.
